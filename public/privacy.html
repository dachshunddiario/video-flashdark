Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Application: Flash Dark • Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • Developer: Augusto Siqueira

1. Information We Collect

Flash Dark operates as a local-first software utility. We collect only what is strictly necessary to perform automated social publishing:

  • OAuth Access Credentials: Temporary API tokens (`access_token`, open ID, public account handle) to authenticate publishing requests.
  • Media & Content: Videos and captions selected by you for publishing. These files reside on your local computer and are only transmitted to platform endpoints during the upload operation.
  • No Tracking / No Reselling: We do not track your browsing activity, collect personal telemetry, or share any personal data with third-party advertisers.

2. How We Use Collected Data

Data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate with TikTok Open API and Meta Graph API.
  • Upload and publish designated video content upon your explicit command.
  • Display scheduled queue status in your local Flash Dark interface.

3. Data Storage & Encryption

All sensitive credentials and tokens are stored locally on your machine within encrypted environment settings. Communications with TikTok and Meta servers use end-to-end TLS/HTTPS encryption.

4. Data Deletion & Rights

You can purge all stored tokens and cached data at any time via Flash Dark's local configuration or by revoking permissions directly from your TikTok / Meta account settings.

5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or data requests, please visit: github.com/augustoSiqueira/video-flashdark.
